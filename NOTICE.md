# Notices and provenance

The DRSR experiment code extends the public HEDN research implementation at
commit `44b79852fdcbed7264ef6b14452b6a9e17ba48e0`.

Upstream project: <https://github.com/qwangwl/HEDN>

The upstream source is not redistributed in this repository. Historical class
and module names are retained in the DRSR extension files to preserve linkage
to the recorded experiment provenance. This naming does not make upstream HEDN
results part of the paper's matched evidence.

SEED, SEED-IV, and FACED remain subject to the access and use conditions of
their respective maintainers. No raw EEG or licensed feature arrays are
included.
